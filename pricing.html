FAQ

Questions, answered

When can I download Coach Ty?

Coach Ty is launching soon on the App Store and Google Play. Both the Free and Premium plans will be available at launch.

Can I really train for both running and lifting?

Yes. Coach Ty builds running plans, lifting plans, or a hybrid plan that intelligently balances both. Combined plans are part of Premium.

Which devices and apps does Coach Ty sync with?

Coach Ty connects with Garmin, COROS, Strava, Apple Health and more — pulling in your watch data and pushing workouts back to supported wearables.

What happens to my synced data?

Data from your connected integrations is securely stored in your Coach Ty account so the AI can personalize and adapt your plan over time. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Premium is a simple $5/month subscription you can cancel whenever you like — you'll keep access through the end of your billing period.
