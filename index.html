How it works

From goal to game day in four steps

Set it up once. Coach Ty handles the rest — and keeps adapting as you go.

1

Pick your goal

Choose running, lifting, or a combination — then tell Coach Ty what you're chasing.

2

Connect your gear

Link Garmin, COROS, Strava or Apple Health so Coach Ty knows where you stand.

3

Get your AI plan

Receive a personalized plan that matches your fitness, schedule, and recovery.

4

Train & adapt

Push workouts to your watch, log how you feel, and watch your plan adjust automatically.
